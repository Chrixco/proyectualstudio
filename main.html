Featured Projects

CCB Building: building in the historic centre with regional timber. Cuenca, Ecuador.

A mixed-use corner building in Cuenca that integrates commercial and residential functions within a compact 180 m² lot. Handcrafted brickwork and locally sourced pinewood define its material expression, while reinterpreted masonry details frame views of Cabogana mountain and create a semi-public threshold in the urban fabric.

Farmacia — Adaptive reuse for community health.

A mid-century pharmacy in Quito transformed into a flexible health hub, weaving clinical services with neighbourhood gathering space and a revived public plaza.

Casa Tres Generaciones — Housing for intergenerational care.

A domestic prototype in Cuenca that blends shared patios, independent suites, and productive gardens to support multigenerational living and collective resilience.

Superblock Cuenca — Streets reclaimed for ecology and collective life.

An urban strategy that reorganises mobility hierarchies in Cuenca’s historic centre, liberating interior streets for public space, ecological corridors, and community-led programming.

PlantMe — Climate Action Meets Social Impact

In partnership with Renderless.eu, PlantMe restores native forests in Sinincay to decarbonise, protect water sources, and fund community-led stewardship.

Un Mundo Mejor — Marching for Water, Life, and Dignity

On 16 September 2025, 100,000 people converged in Cuenca to defend Ecuador’s rivers from extractivist mining. We stood alongside communities to demand a future rooted in water, justice, and collective care.

C3 Climbing Gym – Space as resistance, surface as movement. Offering a new place for climbers in Cuenca, Ecuador.

A climbing centre designed to merge sport and spatial experience, combining verticality, movement, and community engagement. The project creates an indoor landscape that translates climbing routes into architectural surfaces.

Flooded Futures: Guayaquil.

A climate-adaptive vision for Guayaquil’s river delta that explores resilient housing, amphibious infrastructures, and ecological restoration through speculative urban design.

Lessons from Jardim Colombo 🇧🇷

Insights from collaborative work with residents of Jardim Colombo in São Paulo, focusing on informal settlements, collective resilience, and the co-creation of public space.
