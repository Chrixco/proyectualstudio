PROJECT 1: CCB BUILDING
================================================================================
Title:          CCB Building
Subtitle:       Building in the historic centre with regional timber. Cuenca, Ecuador.
Link:           pages/ccbbuilding.html
Image Path:     images/penthouse/Corral_Portfolio_Page_07_Image_0001.jpg
Alt Text:       CCB Building

Context:        Architecture - Timber Architecture

Description:    A mixed-use corner building in Cuenca that integrates commercial
                and residential functions within a compact 180 m² lot. Handcrafted
                brickwork and locally sourced pinewood define its material expression,
                while reinterpreted masonry details frame views of Cabogana mountain
                and create a semi-public threshold in the urban fabric.

Spanish Description:
                Un edificio de uso mixto en una esquina de Cuenca que integra
                funciones comerciales y residenciales en un lote compacto de 180 m².
                La mampostería artesanal y la madera de pino local definen su
                expresión material, mientras que los detalles reinterpretados del
                ladrillo enmarcan vistas al cerro Cabogana y generan un umbral
                semi-público en el tejido urbano.
