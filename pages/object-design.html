Object Design — Form meets function

Designing objects that serve a purpose. Thoughtful. Intentional. Made with care.

Design Philosophy

Objects are not decoration. They are tools. They are extensions of how we live and work. When you design an object, you are designing an experience. A moment. A gesture. We believe that every object can be simple and beautiful at once. That form and function are not opposed. That the best designs disappear into use—you don't think about them, you just live with them.

  • Approach: User-centered design
  • Materials: Sustainable, local where possible
  • Process: Iterative prototyping
  • Goal: Timeless simplicity

Design Process

We start with questions, not answers. What is the problem? Who is using this? How will it be made? We research. We sketch. We build models. We fail. We test. We listen to feedback. The design emerges through this conversation—between the designer, the user, the maker, and the material.

Details matter. But they should serve the whole. Proportions are not accidents. Colors are chosen for a reason. Edges are softened or sharpened with purpose. Every decision ripples through the object. The result should feel inevitable—as if there was no other way it could be.
