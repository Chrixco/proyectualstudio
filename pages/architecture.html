We move away from extractive practices and Eurocentric models—drawing on the places we work, and grounding design in empirical and qualitative exploration.

CCB Building

A mixed-use corner building in Cuenca that integrates commercial and residential functions within a compact 180 m² lot. Handcrafted brickwork and locally sourced pinewood define its material expression, while reinterpreted masonry details frame views of Cabogana mountain and create a semi-public threshold in the urban fabric.

Farmacia Las Morlaquitas

A neighbourhood pharmacy in Cuenca, Ecuador, designed to combine commercial function with architectural identity. The project balances transparency and enclosure through glass façades, brickwork, and concrete walls—connecting the pharmacy to the street while ensuring robustness and climatic comfort.

Casa de Tres Generaciones

Casa de Tres Generaciones is organised around a central courtyard that acts as the heart of family life. The patio, landscaped with trees, flowers, and natural rocks, creates a microclimate and strengthens the connection between interior and exterior spaces. Brick masonry, exposed concrete, and stone define the construction, emphasising robustness and material honesty. Large openings ensure ventilation and daylight, while covered walkways and transitional thresholds mediate between private rooms and communal areas. The project supports intergenerational living by offering independent units connected by the courtyard, where privacy and togetherness coexist.

Living on the Leine

An award-winning residential design engaging with the river Leine's edge conditions and proposing inclusive and adaptable urban housing typologies.

C3 - Climbing Center

A climbing centre designed to merge sport and spatial experience, combining verticality, movement, and community engagement.

Visitor Centre Harz

A public architectural proposal in the Harz region to enhance environmental education, regional identity, and ecological awareness through integrated design.

Cabana PV

A small-scale retreat in the Andean landscape exploring ecological construction methods and spatial intimacy through vernacular references.
